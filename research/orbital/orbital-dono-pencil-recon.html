03 · The hole — "blue search" + title search in PENCIL vs HouseCanary

PENCIL's name comes from the industry term: an O&E / "pencil search." Yet the two report legs that close a real O&E — the recorded title chain and the off-record municipal-lien ("blue") search — are exactly where PENCIL is shallow next to a HouseCanary-grade report.

Gap 1 · Title search

No recorded chain-of-title leg

PENCIL scores feasibility off parcel + auction corpus, but does not yet pull the deed→mortgage→assignment→foreclosure chain. Competitors sell exactly this as a two-owner / O&E report at $45–50 each.

Gap 2 · Blue / municipal lien

Off-record balances invisible

Water/sewer/trash, code-enforcement, open-permit and HOA estoppel balances are not recorded — they survive auction and hit the buyer. PENCIL has zero coverage here; this is the classic post-auction surprise.

Gap 3 · Outcome labeling

FC outcomes pipeline empty

Zero verified foreclosure outcomes in Duval. Without surviving-lien outcomes, the title/blue legs can't be ground-truthed against what actually happened post-sale. [mem: Duval FC outcomes = critical missing piece]

mechanical_lien.scan = FL construction lien · Ch. 713
Inverse of blue — these ARE recorded. Pull recorded Claims of Lien + the Notice of Commencement from Official Records, then compute relation-back priority (§713.07: the lien relates back to the NOC date). Subordinate to a mortgage recorded before the NOC → wiped by the foreclosure; if the NOC predates the foreclosing mortgage → senior → survives. Gotcha: can attach even if the owner paid the GC in full. Enforced in circuit court like a mortgage foreclosure.
source: county Official Records (recorded) · NOC date · feeds lien_survival.classify
STATUS · PARTIAL (build) — def LOCKED ✓ FL construction lien Ch. 713 (VERIFIED); shares title_chain source, needs §713.07 relation-back logic
lien_survival.classify
Must branch on sale_type — survival is opposite across the two.  Foreclosure: recording-date priority — liens junior to the foreclosing mortgage are wiped, senior ones survive (mechanic's lien via §713.07 relation-back).  Tax deed (§197.552): the tax lien is super-priority and wipes essentially ALL private liens regardless of date — mortgages, judgments, HOA/COA, mechanic's. Only governmental liens unsatisfied by proceeds (§197.573), easements/restrictions running with the land, and federal liens (IRS 120-day / SBA 1-yr redemption) survive. So blue_lien.scan is the dominant survival factor for tax deeds; mechanical/mortgage analysis dominates foreclosures.
source: docket + title_chain + sale_type · rules §197.552 / §713.07
