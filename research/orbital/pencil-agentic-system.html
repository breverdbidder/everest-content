Why it is one system, not six tools

lien_survival.classify is the hinge. It consumes Audit + Graph (the recorded chain and the lien edges), branches on sale_type — foreclosure runs recording-date priority, tax deed runs §197.552 super-priority — and emits the survival signal that Influence prices and Convert charges for. Pull that one tool and the loop is just disconnected parts. That is also why a foreclosure and a tax deed can never be modeled apart: their survival math is opposite, so the same parcel carries two different liabilities depending on how it sells.

Substrate honesty

Spec'd end-to-end, but only four phases have real substrate. The loop is closed on paper; the conductive parts are A / G / E / I. N and C are design, and T is hollow exactly where it matters — Duval.
